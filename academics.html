Four Tracks, Every Learner Covered

Choose the Path That Fits Your Future

Every SCB student works toward a nationally recognized certification. Whether your strength is academic theory, hands-on technical skill, business, or technology, there's a structured track built for you.

📘

Track 01

Arts & Science

Our core academic track covers the full range of general arts and science subjects, preparing students for national examinations at both Ordinary and Advanced Level.

Certification: GCE Ordinary Level & GCE Advanced Level

Track 02

Technical

Hands-on, career-ready training in three specialized technical fields:

  • Building & Construction
  • Electrical Power Systems
  • Auto Mobile Mechanics

Certification: GCE ITVE (Intermediate Technical Vocational Education) & GCE TVEE (Technical and Vocational Education Examination, Advanced Level)

🛠️
💼

Track 03

Commercial

A business-focused track equipping students with practical financial and enterprise skills:

  • Accountancy
  • Marketing
  • Home Economics

Certification: GCE Ordinary & Advanced Level (Commercial Subjects)

Track 04

Computer Science

Our newest track prepares students for a digital-first world, covering computing fundamentals and practical ICT skills:

  • Computer Science
  • Information & Communication Technology (ICT)
  • Programming Fundamentals

Certification: GCE Ordinary & Advanced Level (Computer Science)

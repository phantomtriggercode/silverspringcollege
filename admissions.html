How to Apply

Your Path to Enrollment

01

Submit Inquiry

Fill out the inquiry form below or contact our admissions office directly with your child's information.

02

Document Review

Submit required documents: birth certificate, previous school report cards, and passport photographs.

03

Placement

Our academic team reviews the student's records and confirms placement into the appropriate class and track.

04

Confirm Enrollment

Complete registration and fee payment to secure your child's place at SCB for the academic year.

What You'll Need

Admission Requirements

To complete enrollment at Silverspring College Beka, parents and guardians should prepare the following documents:

  • ✔ Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • ✔ Previous school report cards / transcript
  • ✔ 4 passport-sized photographs
  • ✔ Certificate of transfer (if changing schools)
  • ✔ Completed SCB registration form
ℹ️

Have Questions?

Our admissions office is happy to guide you through document requirements specific to your child's entry class. Reach out via the Contact page or the form below.

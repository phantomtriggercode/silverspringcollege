Who We Are

A First-Class Education, Built on Discipline and Purpose

Silverspring College Beka was founded in Beka Matali, Ngaoundéré, by the Enow Njock Foundation to give young Cameroonians access to quality secondary education. We combine rigorous academics with strong moral values, preparing every learner to sit national examinations with confidence and step into higher education or skilled careers with purpose.

Every child who walks through our doors is treated as an achiever — fully equipped to become a changemaker in Ngaoundéré, across Cameroon, and beyond.
